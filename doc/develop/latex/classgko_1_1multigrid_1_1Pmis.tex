Parallel modified independent set (\mbox{\hyperlink{classgko_1_1multigrid_1_1Pmis}{Pmis}}) is the classical coarsening method introduced in the paper H. 

De Sterck et al., \char`\"{}\+Reducing complexity in parallel
algebraic multigrid preconditioners\char`\"{}.

\mbox{\hyperlink{classgko_1_1multigrid_1_1Pmis}{Pmis}} creates the coarse-\/ and fine-\/points group according to the matrix value not the structure.
